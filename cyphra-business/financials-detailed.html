Financial Assumptions

Revenue Assumptions

  • SOC Standard: ₹50K/month flat rate
  • DOC Pro: ₹1.5L/month (includes on-premise support)
  • Full Platform: ₹3L/month + ₹1K per Messenger user
  • Annual contracts with 15% discount for prepayment
  • Customer count grows from 3 (Y1) to 88 (Y5)

COGS Assumptions

  • Cloud hosting: ₹3K-5K/customer/month (on-premise reduces this)
  • Payment processing: 2% of revenue (Razorpay/Stripe India)
  • Customer support: 1 engineer per 15 enterprise customers
  • Gross margin improves from 81% to 92% as fixed costs are amortized

OpEx Assumptions

  • Personnel: Avg CTC ₹5L (Y1) → ₹10L (Y5) — Bangalore market rates
  • Marketing: No paid ads — defence expos, LinkedIn, direct outreach
  • Compliance: ISO 27001 annual maintenance + regular security audits
  • Tax rate: 25.17% (new manufacturing / startup India tax regime)

Timeline Assumptions

  • First customer: Month 6 (SOC Standard)
  • First DOC customer: Month 14 (after certifications)
  • First Full Platform: Month 18 (after defence relationship building)
  • Break-even: Month 28 (cumulative cash positive)
  • No international expansion until Year 5+
