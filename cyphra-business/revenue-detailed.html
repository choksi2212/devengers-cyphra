MRR Growth Trajectory

Growth Strategy & Channels

Defence Expos & Live Demos (40%)

Defexpo, Aero India, DSEI India — live packet capture threat detection demos in real-time. Most convincing sales channel for defence buyers.

Government Tenders (25%)

GeM portal, defence procurement tenders, CERT-In mandated SOC requirements for critical infrastructure. Direct procurement pipeline.

CERT-In & NCIIPC Partnerships (15%)

National cybersecurity body referrals, NCIIPC critical infrastructure protection mandates. Official empanelment creates trusted channel.

Direct Enterprise Sales (15%)

CISO outreach at fintech, banking, telecom companies. POC deployments converting to paid contracts within 60 days.

Academic & Research (5%)

IIT/NIT cybersecurity labs, DRDO research partnerships, published papers and whitepapers building credibility in the Indian security community.

Key Revenue Assumptions

Customer Acquisition

  • Sales cycle: 3-6 months for enterprise, 6-12 months for defence
  • POC to paid conversion: 60% (high because live demos are compelling)
  • Average deal size grows: ₹6L (Y1) → ₹18L (Y5) as customers upgrade tiers
  • Net revenue retention: 120-130% (expansion revenue from tier upgrades + Messenger users)
  • Win rate vs competitors: 40% (Make in India + PQC + on-premise advantage)

Pricing Assumptions

  • Pricing power: 3-5x cheaper than CrowdStrike/Palo Alto while being Indian-made
  • Annual discounts: 15% discount for annual prepayment
  • Messenger per-user pricing: Scales with deployment size (50-500 users per org)
  • No free tier: B2B product — POC is the "trial" period (30-60 days free)
  • Government pricing: May be 10-20% lower due to volume, but longer contracts (3-5 years)

Market Penetration

  • India SOC/SIEM market: ~₹2,500 Cr total (growing 18% CAGR)
  • CYPHRA Year 5 share: 0.5% of total market = ₹12 Cr
  • Defence allocation: ~30% of revenue from govt/defence by Year 3
  • Geographic focus: Bangalore, Delhi NCR, Hyderabad, Pune (major IT + defence hubs)
  • No international expansion until Year 5+ — India market is large enough
