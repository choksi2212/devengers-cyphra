Metric Definitions

Key Formula Reference

  • LTV = ARPU × Gross Margin × (1 / Monthly Churn Rate)
  • CAC = Total Sales & Marketing Spend / New Customers Acquired
  • CAC Payback = CAC / (ARPU × Gross Margin) in months
  • Net Revenue Retention = (Starting MRR + Expansion - Contraction - Churn) / Starting MRR
  • Magic Number = Net New ARR (this quarter) / Sales & Marketing Spend (last quarter)
  • Rule of 40 = Revenue Growth Rate % + EBITDA Margin %
  • Logo Churn = Customers Lost / Total Customers at Start of Period
