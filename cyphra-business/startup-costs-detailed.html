Cost Optimization Strategy

Infrastructure Efficiency

  • On-premise deployments reduce cloud costs
  • Rust backend = 10x lower compute than Python/Java equivalents
  • VedDB custom database = zero licensing fees
  • Self-hosted monitoring (no DataDog/New Relic fees)
  • AWS Activate startup credits (up to $100K)

Marketing Efficiency

  • Defence expos provide direct buyer access (no funnel needed)
  • Live threat detection demos are the most convincing sales tool
  • GeM portal provides government procurement pipeline
  • Zero paid advertising — 100% B2B direct outreach
  • Technical blog/whitepaper content for organic credibility

Operational Efficiency

  • 2-person founding team (no bloated headcount)
  • Remote-first culture (minimal office costs)
  • Open-source tooling wherever possible
  • Revenue-funded growth after Month 7
  • No unnecessary SaaS subscriptions
