Year 1 Expense Breakdown

Personnel — ₹10 L (53%)

2 founders (₹25K/mo each) + freelance contractors for UI/UX polish and penetration testing. No full-time hires until Year 2.

Marketing & BD — ₹3 L (16%)

Defence expo attendance (Defexpo), LinkedIn B2B campaigns, GeM portal registration, pitch deck production. Zero paid advertising.

Infrastructure — ₹2 L (10%)

AWS Mumbai production servers, Cloudflare CDN, CI/CD pipeline, monitoring stack. Rust backend = ultra-low compute costs.

Compliance — ₹2.5 L (13%)

ISO 27001 preparation, 3rd party pentest, CERT-In empanelment application, DPDP compliance documentation. Critical for B2B trust.

Cost Optimization Strategy

Why Costs Are Low

  • Rust backend: 10x lower compute than Java/Python equivalents — server costs stay minimal
  • VedDB custom database: Zero licensing fees vs ₹10-50L/year for commercial databases
  • On-premise model: Customers pay for their own hardware — CYPHRA has minimal hosting costs
  • No paid advertising: B2B cybersecurity sells through demos, not Google Ads
  • Remote-first: No expensive office leases — co-working only for client meetings
