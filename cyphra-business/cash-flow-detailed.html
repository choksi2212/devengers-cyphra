Cash Flow Milestones

M1

Foundation & Setup

Month 0–3 | Cash: -₹5.8L
  • Heavy upfront spending: Company registration, servers, certifications
  • Zero revenue — pure investment phase
  • Burn rate: ₹1.5-2.5L/month
M2

First Revenue & POC

Month 4–8 | Cash: -₹9.4L (trough)
  • First POC deployed (Month 5), first paid contract (Month 6)
  • Revenue starts: ₹0.5L → ₹1L/month
  • Cash burn slows dramatically — monthly break-even by Month 8
M3

Revenue Growth

Month 9–18 | Cash recovering
  • 3 SOC customers generating ₹1.5L/month
  • Positive monthly cash flow from Month 9 onwards
  • iDEX grant application (potential ₹50L boost)
M4

Break-Even Point

Month 28 | Cumulative cash turns positive
  • Cumulative cash flow crosses ₹0 — all initial investment recovered
  • MRR exceeds ₹10L/month
  • 13+ paying customers across tiers
M5

Profitable Scale

Month 29–60 | Cash surplus growing
  • Net profit margin: 29-34%
  • Free cash flow: ₹2.8-4.8 Cr annually
  • Self-funded growth — no external capital needed

Funding Scenarios

Scenario 1: Bootstrap Only

Capital: ₹15 Lakhs (founder savings)

Runway: 14 months at worst

Break-Even: Month 28

Risk: Tight — any delays in first customer push break-even back

Equity Dilution: 0%

Best For: If first 3 customers land within 6 months

Scenario 2: Bootstrap + iDEX Grant

Capital: ₹15L + ₹50L grant = ₹65L

Runway: 30+ months (comfortable)

Break-Even: Month 22 (faster hiring)

Risk: Low — large buffer for govt sales cycle delays

Equity Dilution: 0% (grant, not equity)

Best For: DOC module targeting defence customers

Scenario 3: Bootstrap + Seed Round

Capital: ₹15L + ₹1Cr seed = ₹1.15 Cr

Runway: 36+ months

Break-Even: Month 18 (aggressive hiring + sales)

Risk: Very low — but 10% equity dilution

Equity Dilution: 8-12%

Best For: Rapid enterprise market capture
